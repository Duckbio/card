Tomato Soup I made for Molly when she was ill :)

Ingredients

  • About 12 medium-sized tomatoes, or like 500 grams of tomatoes
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 1 celery stick, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1L vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Basil
  • Salt and pepper :p

Method

  1. In a large saucepan, add the onions, carrots and celery. Cover and cook on medium heat for 10 min, stirring every few min
  2. Add the garlic at the end and let fry for another minute
  3. Slice the tomatoes into quarters and halves, add them and 1 tsp of sugar, salt to eye and pepper, stir and cook for 10 min.
  4. Add all the stock and simmer for 10-20 minutes, until reduced.
  5. Blend until smooth.
  6. Add basil, and even parmesan, when serving.
