My fav pasta recipe I made up

Ingredients

  • Pasta, eyeball
  • Cherry tomatoes, eyeball
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan (optional)

Method

  1. Boil pasta in a pan, with just enough water to cover pasta.
  2. Once al dente, place pasta and strachy water seperately
  3. Fry cherry tomatoes on medium heat and cover, wait for about 3 min
  4. Add garlic and salt
  5. Once the tomatoes have burst, or are about to burst, turn heat to high
  6. Throw in pasta and the starchy water and stir like a motherfucker
  7. Let reduce for a bit if needed and add a lil more salt and pepper.
